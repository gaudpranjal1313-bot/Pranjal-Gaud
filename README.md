# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview

This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Power BI. The objective was to analyze sales performance, profitability, discounts, product categories, and regional trends to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard enables users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify high and low-performing areas, and support data-driven business decisions.

Project Objectives

- Analyze category-wise sales performance and trends.
- Examine the relationship between sales, discounts, and profit.
- Identify high and low-performing products.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns across regions.
- Evaluate sales and profitability across different business dimensions.
- Generate actionable insights to support business decision-making.

Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks important KPIs including:

- Total Sales: ₹1.32M
- Total Profit: ₹391.47K
- Profit Margin: 29.55%
- Average Discount: 20%

Key Insights

- Analyzed sales and profitability across different product categories and regions.
- Identified variations in regional and category-level performance.
- Evaluated the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Identified high and low-performing products to highlight potential areas for improvement.
- Improved visibility of key sales metrics through interactive Power BI visualizations.

Business Value

This analysis demonstrates how business data can be transformed into actionable insights using Power BI. The dashboard can help decision-makers monitor performance, identify trends, evaluate profitability, and recognize opportunities for business growth.
